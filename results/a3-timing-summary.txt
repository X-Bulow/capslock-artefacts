A3 timing summary
=================

Timing host: separate x86-64 machine
Workload: 100 compression/decompression iterations per measurement
Repetitions: 5 per variant
Statistic: arithmetic mean of the 5 measurements

Mean wall-clock times
---------------------
native x86-64:                                      0.014996 s
stock qemu-riscv64, unoptimised RISC-V guest:       0.170234 s
CapsLock QEMU, capability operations stubbed/no-op: 1.456172 s
full CapsLock QEMU:                                32.230098 s

Calculated ratios
-----------------
stock / native:  11.351961x
stub / native:   97.104028x
full / native: 2149.246332x
stub / stock:     8.553943x
full / stub:     22.133442x

Absolute-slowdown decomposition
-------------------------------
full - native: 32.215102 s
full - stub:   30.773926 s

(full - stub) / (full - native): 95.526396%
(stub - native) / (full - native): 4.473604%

Interpretation
--------------
The first percentage is the portion of full-over-native elapsed-time
increase added when full checking is enabled over the no-op CapsLock
configuration. The complement includes CapsLock QEMU and instrumented-guest
overhead as well as emulation, so it is not a pure cross-ISA emulation figure.
